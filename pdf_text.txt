   NARRATIVE REPORT ON THE PROCUREMENT OF MILK UNDER THE MILK FEEDING 
COMPONENT
 
OF
 
THE
 
DEPED’S
 
SCHOOL-BASED
 
FEEDING
 
PROGRAM
  
As part of the implementation of the Milk Feeding Component of the Department of 
Education’s
 
(DepEd)
 
School-Based
 
Feeding
 
Program
 
(SBFP)
,
 
pursuant
 
to
 
Republic
 
Act
 
No.
 
11037,
 
or
 
the
 
Masustansyang
 
Pagkain
 
para
 
sa
 
Batang
 
Pilipino
 
Act
,
 
and
 
funded
 
under
 
the
 
FY
 
2026
 
General
 
Appropriations
 
Act
 
(GAA)
,
 
the
 
Philippine
 
Carabao
 
Center
 
National
 
Headquarters
 
and
 
Gene
 
Pool
 
(PCC
 
NHQ),
 
together
 
with
 
its
 
twelve
 
(12)
 
Regional
 
Centers,
 
is
 
undertaking
 
the
 
procurement
 
and
 
delivery
 
of
 
milk
 
to
 
support
 
the
 
feeding
 
requirements
 
of
 
identified
 
Schools
 
Division
 
Offices
 
(SDOs).
 
The procurement is being undertaken in accordance with applicable government procurement 
laws
 
and
 
regulations.
 
In
 
particular,
 
as
 
per
 
Republic
 
Act
 
No.
 
11321,
 
or
 
the
 
Sagip
 
Saka
 
Act
,
 
the
 
direct
 
procurement
 
of
 
agricultural
 
and
 
fishery
 
products
 
from
 
accredited
 
farmers
 
and
 
fisherfolk
 
cooperatives
 
and
 
organizations
 
is
 
prioritized.
 
The
 
use
 
of
 
the
 
Sagip
 
Saka
 
procurement
 
mechanism
 
supports
 
the
 
participation
 
of
 
qualified
 
dairy
 
cooperatives
 
and
 
farmer
 
organizations
 
assisted
 
by
 
the
 
PCC,
 
thereby
 
providing
 
a
 
stable
 
market
 
for
 
locally
 
produced
 
carabao
 
milk
 
while
 
contributing
 
to
 
the
 
livelihood
 
and
 
income
 
of
 
dairy
 
farmers.
 
In coordination with the concerned SDOs, PCC NHQ and its Regional Centers are facilitating the 
procurement
 
and
 
delivery
 
of
 
the
 
required
 
milk
 
products
 
up
 
to
 
the
 
designated
 
drop-off
 
points
 
to
 
ensure
 
that
 
these
 
are
 
available
 
in
 
accordance
 
with
 
the
 
respective
 
feeding
 
schedules
 
and
 
the
 
school
 
calendar
 
prescribed
 
by
 
DepEd.
 
The
 
implementation
 
seeks
 
to
 
provide
 
identified
 
beneficiaries
 
with
 
safe,
 
quality,
 
and
 
nutritious
 
milk
 
while
 
simultaneously
 
strengthening
 
the
 
local
 
dairy
 
value
 
chain
 
and
 
creating
 
a
 
sustainable
 
market
 
for
 
milk
 
produced
 
by
 
PCC-assisted
 
dairy
 
cooperatives.
 
A total of ninety-three (93) SDOs are covered by the procurement and supply of pasteurized 
and
 
sterilized
 
milk
 
through
 
PCC
 
NHQ
 
and
 
its
 
Regional
 
Centers.
 
Each
 
SDO
 
selected
 
its
 
preferred
 
delivery
 
batch
 
based
 
on
 
its
 
feeding
 
schedule
 
and
 
the
 
DepEd
 
school
 
calendar.
 
Batch
 
1
 
covers
 
August
 
to
 
September
 
2026,
 
Batch
 
2
 
covers
 
September
 
to
 
November
 
2026,
 
Batch
 
3
 
covers
 
November
 
2026
 
to
 
February
 
2027,
 
and
 
Batch
 
4
 
covers
 
February
 
to
 
March
 
2027.
 
This
 
batching
 
scheme
 
was
 
established
 
to
 
synchronize
 
the
 
procurement,
 
production,
 
and
 
delivery
 
of
 
milk
 
with
 
the
 
scheduled
 
feeding
 
periods
 
of
 
the
 
covered
 
SDOs
 
and
 
to
 
facilitate
 
the
 
efficient
 
and
 
timely
 
implementation
 
of
 
the
 
program.
 
Table 1 presents the list of SDOs covered by the milk procurement, including their respective 
delivery
 
batches
 
and
 
other
 
relevant
 
procurement
 
details.
 
As of August 18, 2026, a total of 179,487 packs of milk have been delivered to two (2) SDOs. 
Delivery
 
to
 
the
 
SDO
 
of
 
Science
 
City
 
of
 
Muñoz
 
and
 
Mati
 
City
 
is
 
currently
 
ongoing
 
and
 
is
 
expected
 
to
 
be
 
completed
 
by
 
September
 
2026
.
 
The
 
procurement
 
of
 
milk
 
for
 
seven
 
(
7)
 
SDOs
 
has
 
already
 
been
 
awarded
 
and
 
is
 
awaiting
 
delivery,
 
while
 
procurement
 
for
 
twenty-five
 
(25)
  

   SDOs is currently ongoing. Meanwhile, the procurement for seventy-eight (78)  SDOs is in the 
preparatory
 
stage.
 
The PCC National Headquarters and Gene Pool (PCC NHQ) and its twelve (12) Regional Centers 
continue
 
to
 
closely
 
coordinate
 
with
 
the
 
concerned
 
SDOs
 
and
 
qualified
 
suppliers
 
to
 
facilitate
 
the
 
completion
 
of
 
procurement
 
activities
 
and
 
ensure
 
the
 
timely
 
delivery
 
of
 
milk
 
in
 
accordance
 
with
 
the
 
approved
 
feeding
 
schedules
 
and
 
the
 
DepEd
 
school
 
calendar.
