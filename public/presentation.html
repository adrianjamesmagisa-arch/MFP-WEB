Operations Department Training & Overview

Why Move Away from Excel?

Fragmented Data

Multiple files spread across different centers, making it impossible to get a real-time nationwide overview.

Formula Errors

Accidental overwrites of manual formulas causing inaccurate data and broken reports.

Introducing MFP-WEB

A secure, cloud-based platform custom-built for our operations.

Centralized Database: All centers input data into one secure, unified system.
Bulletproof Formulas: Calculations are locked and handled by the system automatically. No more broken cells.
Real-Time Dashboard: Instant insights and metrics at the click of a button.

System Highlights

  • Role-Based Access: Encoders only see their center's data; Super Admins see everything.
  • Smart Data Entry: Sticky columns, auto-suggest dropdowns for suppliers, and spreadsheet-like speed.
  • Bulk Editing: Select multiple records from the Masterlist and update them all at once.
  • Built-in Validation: The system automatically warns you about duplicate records.

How to Use: Dashboard

Your new command center for tracking the program.

Login: Access the system using your secure credentials. You will land on the Dashboard.
Filter Data: Use the dropdowns at the top right to filter the metrics by Year, Month, or Center.
Review Metrics: Instantly view total volume requirements, total funds transferred, and milk cost breakdowns across the country.

How to Use: Add Records

Entering data is just as fast as Excel, but much smarter.

Navigate: Click "Add Records" on the left sidebar.
Fill the Rows: Type across the row just like a spreadsheet. Sticky columns (A to F) will follow you as you scroll right.
Auto-Calculations: Yellow columns are calculated automatically (e.g., Milk Packs = Beneficiaries × Feeding Days). You don't need to touch them!
Smart Suppliers: In the Supplier column, simply start typing the name of the Cooperative to filter the dropdown.

How to Use: Bulk Edit

Need to update 10 records at once? No problem.

Select Records: Go to the "Masterlist" page. Use the checkboxes on the left to select the records you want to update.
Click Bulk Edit: Click the "Bulk Edit" button that appears at the top.
Update & Save: You will be taken to a familiar spreadsheet view containing only your selected records. Make your changes and click Save.
