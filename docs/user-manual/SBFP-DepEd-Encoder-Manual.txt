SBFP (DepEd) — Encoder User Manual
==================================
Philippine Carabao Center · MFP Web Application

Dear Encoder,

Thank you for your patience and care in updating School-Based Feeding Program
(SBFP) data for your center. When your work is funded by **DepEd**, please use
the **SBFP** section of the application (not DSWD monitoring).

This guide explains:
  • How to open your center and school year
  • What you should type vs. what the system calculates
  • Important buttons (deliveries, add SDO, add school)
  • Formulas used for auto-calculated cells


1. GETTING STARTED
------------------
1. Sign in with your encoder account.
2. In the sidebar, click **SBFP**.
3. Choose the **school year** (e.g. 2025–2026), then open your **center** page.
   • Most centers: SBFP → Center → [Your center name] → [Year]
   • NHQ / NIZ staff: SBFP → **NHQ** hub (same operational center as NHQGP (NIZ))
4. At the top you will see summary tiles (SDOs, target packs, delivery progress).
   These are **information only** — you do not type into them.


2. SECTION 1 — SDO PROCUREMENT
------------------------------
One row per **School Division Office (SDO)**. Columns on the left stay visible
when you scroll sideways (Status, SDO name).

2.1 Buttons (please use these as intended)
-------------------------------------------
• **Add delivered-as-of date**
  - Click the button, then pick a **calendar date** from the date picker.
  - A new column group appears, labeled like “P — Delivered as of Aug. 18, 2026.”
  - In that column you enter **cumulative packs delivered as of that date**
    (running total up to that day, not “packs delivered only on that day”).
  - You may add **more than one** date column as deliveries progress.
  - To change the label date, use the small edit control on the column header
    (if your role allows). To remove a column, use the delete control on the
    header — you will be asked to confirm; packs stored under that date are cleared.

• **Add SDO**
  - Adds a new procurement row for another SDO when your center covers a new division.
  - Please complete Region, Amount, milk type, and procurement fields after adding.

• **Row checkbox + bulk actions** (when shown)
  - Select rows for bulk operations your coordinator has enabled.

• **Actions (trash icon)** on a row
  - Deletes that SDO row. Please confirm only when the row was added by mistake.

2.2 What YOU should input (editable cells)
------------------------------------------
| Column / area              | What to enter |
|----------------------------|---------------|
| In Report?                 | Check if this SDO should appear in reports |
| A — Status                 | Procurement status (e.g. For Preparation, Ongoing, Awarded, Done) |
| B — SDO                    | Division name |
| C — Region                 | Region code (CAR, III, NCR, etc.) |
| D — Amount (₱)             | DepEd allocation / contract amount in pesos |
| Milk type                  | PM, SM, or CM |
| Pack ₱                     | **Only for CM (Commercial milk)** — pesos per pack. PM and SM use fixed prices (see below). |
| E — Mode of Procurement    | e.g. Bayanihan Act, NP-CP, etc. |
| Coop                       | Cooperative supplying this SDO (syncs to schools under this SDO on the masterlist) |
| F — Date Recd (Proc)       | Date documents received for procurement |
| G — PR Number              | Purchase request number |
| H — ORS Date               | Obligation request date |
| I — PO Number              | Purchase order number |
| J — Batch                  | Batch identifier |
| K — Beneficiaries          | Number of beneficiaries (SDO level, if used on your row) |
| L — Contract Amt (₱)       | Contract amount if different from Amount |
| M — Delivery Start         | Start of delivery (also updates linked drop-off schools on the masterlist) |
| N — Delivery End           | End of delivery (also updates linked schools) |
| Delivered-as-of columns    | **Cumulative packs delivered** as of each date you added |
| Raw ₱/L (per month)        | Raw milk price per liter for that calendar month (when the month column is visible) |
| Payment Status, Remarks    | As needed for your center’s workflow |

**Note:** Column **O — Packs to Deliver** is usually **calculated** from Amount
and milk type (see formulas). Your role may still allow a manual override — please
use overrides only when your coordinator instructs you to.

2.3 What the system calculates (please do not re-type these unless overriding)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| Column / area              | How it is computed |
|----------------------------|--------------------|
| Pack ₱ (PM / SM)           | Fixed: PM = ₱25, SM = ₱30 (display only; you do not type) |
| O — Packs to Deliver       | See formula **F1** below |
| Raw milk used (L)          | See formula **F2** (per visible month) |
| Income (per month)         | See formula **F3** |
| Total Income               | Sum of each month’s Income (only packs **completed in that month**) |
| Dashboard “Target milk packs” / “Delivery progress %” | From sums of Packs to Deliver and delivered packs across SDOs |

**When do monthly Raw milk / Income columns appear?**
After you add at least one **Delivered as-of** date (or when delivery data exists
for feeding months Aug–Dec of the school year start year), the system shows
month groups (August, September, …). Packs for each month are derived from your
delivered-as-of snapshots (see **F4**).

2.4 Formulas (SBFP procurement)
-------------------------------
F1 — Packs to Deliver (column O)
  Let Amount = D — Amount (₱).
  Let Pack ₱ =
    • PM (Pasteurized)  → 25
    • SM (Sterilized)   → 30
    • CM (Commercial)   → value you entered in Pack ₱
  If Amount > 0 and Pack ₱ > 0:
    Packs to Deliver = round( Amount ÷ Pack ₱ )

F2 — Raw milk utilized (liters) for a month
  Let P = packs **completed in that calendar month** (see F4).
  Raw milk used (L) = ( P ÷ 5 ) × 0.20

F3 — Raw milk income for a month
  Let P = packs completed in that month (F4).
  Let R = Raw ₱/L you entered for that month.
  Income = Raw milk used (L) × R = ( (P ÷ 5) × 0.20 ) × R

F4 — Packs completed in one month (from “Delivered as-of” columns)
  For each month, the system looks at all “Delivered as-of” dates in that month,
  takes the **maximum cumulative packs** shown on those dates in the month, and
  subtracts the maximum cumulative packs from **before** that month:
    Month increment = max(cumulative packs in month) − max(cumulative packs before month)
  (If you enter explicit monthly pack fields elsewhere, those take precedence.)


3. SECTION 1b — DROP-OFF POINTS (SCHOOLS)
-----------------------------------------
Schools under each SDO. Add schools only **after** the parent SDO exists in
Section 1.

3.1 Buttons
-----------
• **Add school** — Pick the SDO, then add the school / drop-off name.
• Row checkbox — For bulk delete when enabled.

3.2 What YOU should input
-------------------------
| Field              | What to enter |
|--------------------|---------------|
| SDO (parent)       | Link school to the correct SDO row |
| Drop-off / School  | School name |
| Beneficiaries      | Number of children / beneficiaries |
| Feeding days       | Days of feeding (e.g. 120) — often from SDO or standard options |
| Other remarks      | If your table includes editable remark columns |

3.3 Auto-calculated (drop-off → masterlist / MFP)
-------------------------------------------------
When beneficiaries and feeding days are both greater than zero, the system
computes milk needs using **F5–F9** (same family as MFP Data formulations).
These values sync to the DepEd school masterlist — **please do not type over
calculated pack/volume fields** unless your coordinator says otherwise.

F5 — Milk packs (drop-off)
  Milk packs = Beneficiaries × Feeding days

F6 — Total volume (liters)
  Liters per pack depends on milk type from the parent SDO:
    • SM (Sterilized)     → 0.18 L per pack (180 ml)
    • PM / CM / default   → 0.20 L per pack (200 ml)
  Total volume (L) = Milk packs × Liters per pack

F7 — Raw milk (kg equivalent path uses volume)
  Raw milk = Total volume (L) × 0.20

F8 — Whole milk powder, skim milk powder
  Whole milk powder = Raw milk × 0.268
  Skim milk powder    = Raw milk × 0.274

F9 — Sugar
  Sugar = Total volume (L) × 0.02


4. OTHER SECTIONS ON THE SBFP CENTER PAGE
-----------------------------------------
• **PPMP tables** (Office Supplies, Fixtures/ICT, Training) — enter fund lines
  as your finance team directs.
• **Staff hiring** — enter hiring rows if your center uses this table.
• **2. Center Budget (A–F)** — budget form fields your coordinator assigns.
• **3. Milk Capacity** — capacity fields; some summaries compare to total
  Packs to Deliver from Section 1.

Please treat calculated totals on those forms as **read-only** unless the cell
is clearly editable.


5. MFP DATA (DEPED ROWS)
------------------------
DepEd-funded schools often appear in **MFP Data** after drop-off sync. Volume
and formulation columns there use the same **F5–F9** logic. Encoder edits
should focus on identifiers, dates, beneficiaries, and feeding days — not on
duplicating formula results in Excel elsewhere.


6. SBFP REPORT (SIDEBAR → REPORTS)
-----------------------------------
The **SBFP Report** page reads the same SDO rows you encode under SBFP → your
center → Section 1. You do not re-type data there. After saving in the center
table, open SBFP Report, choose the same school year, and set **Delivered As Of**
to match the delivery snapshot date you need. Rows unchecked for **In Report?**
 are hidden unless an administrator enables “Show SDOs not marked In Report?”


7. POLITE REMINDERS
-------------------
• Please save by clicking out of a cell or pressing Enter; the app saves in the
  background. A brief spinner is normal.
• If a column is gray or shows a fixed ₱ for PM/SM, you do not need to type there.
• For DSWD-funded areas, kindly use **DSWD** monitoring instead of SBFP
  (see DSWD-Encoder-Manual.txt).
• When unsure, leave Remarks noting your question rather than guessing amounts.

Thank you again for supporting PCC and DepEd feeding programs.
