MMSU — Encoder Welcome
======================

Dear colleague at Mariano Marcos State University (MMSU),

Thank you for encoding MFP monitoring data for **MMSU**.

WHICH MODULE TO USE
-------------------
• **DepEd** feeding (School-Based Feeding Program) → open **SBFP** in the sidebar.
• **DSWD** feeding (Supplementary Feeding Program) → open **DSWD** under Program monitoring.

Please do not enter DepEd schools in DSWD, or DSWD municipalities in SBFP.

YOUR TYPICAL PATHS (after sign-in)
----------------------------------
• SBFP (DepEd):  SBFP → MMSU → choose school year
• DSWD:          DSWD → MMSU → choose calendar year
