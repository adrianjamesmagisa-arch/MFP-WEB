DSWD Monitoring — Encoder User Manual
=====================================
Philippine Carabao Center · MFP Web Application

Dear Encoder,

Thank you for maintaining DSWD Supplementary Feeding Program monitoring data.
When your work is funded by **DSWD**, please use **DSWD** under **Program
monitoring** in the sidebar — not the SBFP (DepEd) module.

This guide mirrors the SBFP layout (procurement + municipalities) but uses
**Province** instead of SDO and **Municipality** instead of school.


1. GETTING STARTED
------------------
1. Sign in with your encoder account.
2. Sidebar → **Program monitoring** → **DSWD**.
3. Select the **calendar year**, then open your **center** page.
   Example path pattern: /monitoring/dswd/center/[YourCenter]/[Year]
4. Summary tiles (provinces, target packs, delivery progress) are **read-only**.


2. SECTION 1 — PROCUREMENT STATUS
---------------------------------
One row per **Province** (or area label your center uses).

2.1 Buttons
-----------
• **Add delivered-as-of date**
  - Same behavior as SBFP: pick a date, then enter **cumulative packs delivered
    as of that date** in the new column.
  - Multiple dates may be added over time; header controls rename or delete
    a date column when allowed.

• **Add province** (or equivalent add-row control on your screen)
  - Creates a new procurement row for another province/area.

• Row delete / bulk actions — Only when your role permits; please confirm deletes.

2.2 What YOU should input
-------------------------
The columns match SBFP SDO procurement (Status, Region, Amount, milk type,
mode of procurement, coop, procurement dates and numbers, beneficiaries,
contract amount, delivery start/end, delivered-as-of packs, Raw ₱/L, payment
status, remarks). Replace “SDO” mentally with **Province**:

| You type                         | Notes |
|----------------------------------|-------|
| In Report?, Status, Province, Region | Identity and procurement stage |
| Amount (₱), Milk type, Pack ₱ (CM only) | Pack ₱ fixed for PM/SM |
| Mode, Coop, PR/PO dates & numbers | Procurement trail |
| Delivery Start / End             | Syncs to municipalities under this province |
| Delivered-as-of columns          | Cumulative packs per date |
| Raw ₱/L per month                | When month block is visible |
| Payment status, Remarks          | As needed |

2.3 Auto-calculated
-------------------
| Item | Formula |
|------|---------|
| Pack ₱ for PM | ₱25 (fixed, not typed) |
| Pack ₱ for SM | ₱30 (fixed, not typed) |
| Packs to Deliver | **F1** — same as SBFP: round(Amount ÷ Pack ₱) |
| Raw milk used (L) per month | **F2**: (P ÷ 5) × 0.20 where P = packs completed that month |
| Income per month | **F3**: Raw milk used (L) × Raw ₱/L |
| Total Income | Sum of monthly incomes |
| Delivery progress % | Delivered packs ÷ target packs (summary tile) |

F4 (monthly packs from delivered-as-of columns) is the same incremental logic
described in the SBFP manual.


3. SECTION 1b — DROP-OFF POINTS (MUNICIPALITIES)
------------------------------------------------
Municipalities under each province.

3.1 Buttons
-----------
• **Add municipality** — Select parent province/area, then add the municipality name.

3.2 What YOU should input
-------------------------
| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| Province (parent) | Must match a row from Section 1 |
| Municipality      | Name of municipality / drop-off |
| Beneficiaries     | Count of beneficiaries |
| Feeding days      | Number of feeding days |

3.3 Auto-calculated (sync to MFP Data)
--------------------------------------
Same formulation family as SBFP schools (**F5–F9**):

  F5  Milk packs = Beneficiaries × Feeding days
  F6  Total volume (L) = Milk packs × (0.18 if SM, else 0.20 L/pack)
  F7  Raw milk = Total volume × 0.20
  F8  Whole powder = Raw milk × 0.268; Skim powder = Raw milk × 0.274
  F9  Sugar = Total volume × 0.02

MFP Data rows for DSWD use **Funded By = DSWD** and show **N/A** for DepEd
Division and Elementary School columns — that is expected.


4. REPORTS
----------
• **PIMD report** link on the center page filters by your center and year.
• **Summary DSWD** in the sidebar aggregates DSWD monitoring for reporting.

Please encode monitoring tables first; reports read from your saved data.


5. DEPED vs DSWD — QUICK CHOICE
-------------------------------
| Funder | Module in sidebar | Primary geography |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| DepEd  | SBFP              | SDO → Schools     |
| DSWD   | DSWD              | Province → Municipalities |

Thank you for your careful encoding. If a municipality or province is missing,
please add the parent procurement row first, then add drop-offs.
